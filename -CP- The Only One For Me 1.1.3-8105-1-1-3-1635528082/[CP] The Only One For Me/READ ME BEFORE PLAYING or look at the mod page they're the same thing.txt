Overview

This mod provides a story to spice up year one in Pelican Town. This mod DOES NOT contain marriage dialogue or schedule changes at this time. It is largely focused on being a dialogue & event mod. Sebastian has dialogue for every day at every 2 heart levels per season. There is also set dialogue for him, Sam, Abigail, Haley, Alex, and Robin on certain days of the year. These are typically LONGER than a usual conversation in SDV, so please keep that in mind! Sebastian also has new dialogue for before each festival, during each festival, and after each festival. Most other characters also have post-day festival dialogue as well! 
Please see the below section for a guide as to which set days have dialogues and events!

-------------------------------------------------------
Sebastian Overhauls

Sebastian goes from incredibly shy and nervous to a downright horny bastard. Really honed in on the nerdy and socially inept/awkward parts of Sebastian's character. Basically basement dwelling virgin Sebastian. You're welcome?
Here are the list of events:
4 heart event - Impossible to miss
Spring 20 - 7pm - 11pm at the Mountain
Summer 24 - 12pm - 5pm at the Beach
6 hearts and also 6 hearts with Abigail - Also impossible to miss; BE SURE TO HAVE THE FIRST HOUSE UPGRADE BY THIS POINT!
Summer 26 - 8pm - 11pm at the Town
Secret 10 Heart event - Go to the most unlikely places for Seb to be
List of specific day dialogue:
Spring: 12, 14, 23, 25
Summer: 4, 10, 12, 17, 20, 27
Fall: 7, 13, 15, 21, 26
Winter: 7, 9, 15, 19, 24, 28
Each season has it's own 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10 heart daily dialogues. (Which are recycled weekly, just kinda how SDV dialogue works.)

Sam Overhauls

Sam is basically the farmer's best friend in this case. Enthusiastic music dork. I wanted to flesh him out a bit and give him more of a really chipper best friend vibe. A bit of a shit-head though, can't take that away from him.
He has one solo event with the farmer:
Spring 18: 10am to 7pm at Sam's House
List of specific day dialogue:
Spring: 10, 25
Summer: 1, 8, 12, 15, 22, 26
Fall: 6, 14, 17, 28
Winter: 24

Abigail Overhauls
